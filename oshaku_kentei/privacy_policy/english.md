# Privacy Policy

This page is the privacy policy of the smartphone application "Oshaku Kentei" (the "App") developed and operated by mozkzki. It is intended to inform you of the policy regarding the collection, use and disclosure of user information when using the App.

If you choose to use the App, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

**Information Collection and Use**

The App uses Google AdMob for ad delivery. The advertising identifier is obtained only if you allow it through App Tracking Transparency. If you do not allow it, you can still use every feature of the App. The advertising identifier is not used to identify individuals.

The App's rankings use Apple Game Center. If you take part in the rankings, your score and your Game Center nickname are shown to other players through Apple. You can use the App without signing in to Game Center.

Please refer to the privacy policy of the third party service providers below for details such as information to be acquired.

* Google AdMob
* Apple Game Center

In addition, in the support of the App, we may collect your e-mail address and inquiry details. We will use this information to reply to inquiries and improve the quality of the App.

**Use of Device Features**

The App uses the following device features to play. This information is used only on your device, or between your device and one other device nearby. It is never sent to our servers (we do not run any servers).

* Accelerometer and gyroscope (motion sensors): to judge how you tilt the device to pour or receive beer
* Local network: when two people play, the App connects directly to one other device nearby. Your device name is shown on the other device
* Nearby Interaction: when two people play, the App measures the distance between the two devices

Your score, rank and title are calculated on your device. You choose whether to share the certificate image.

**Log Data**

In a case of an error in the App, Log Data may be collected through third-party services (including Apple). This Log Data may include information such as your device name, operating system version, the configuration of the App, the time and date of your use of the App, and other statistics.

**Children's Privacy**

In the case I discover that a child under 13 has provided me with personal information, I immediately delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do the necessary actions.
