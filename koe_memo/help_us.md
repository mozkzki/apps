#### Hands-Free Mode

This is a mode for taking notes repeatedly by voice operation without touching the iPhone/iPad.

##### Preparation for use

First, you need to add the Siri shortcut for "Voice Widget Memo" by the following method.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>How to add Siri Shortcut</summary>
<br>

- Tap "Hands-Free Mode" in the app's settings menu

![add_s_1](images/us/add_siri_1.png)

- Tap "Add to Siri"

![add_s_2](images/us/add_siri_2.png)

- Set your favorite voice phrase and tap "Done" (Default is "Voice Memo")

![add_s_3](images/us/add_siri_3.png)

</details>

##### How to Use

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Usage Flow</summary>
* Device must be unlocked
<br>
<br>

1. Say "Hey Siri, Voice Memo" to your iPhone/iPad (Match the second half with the voice phrase you set)
2. The app will start in recording mode, so record what you want to make a note of
3. Say "Finish" when you're done taking notes
4. The recording ends and the app hides in the background
5. If you want to make additional notes, repeat from step 1.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Tips</summary>

- By default, the content recorded in Hands-Free Mode is saved in "Home Screen: Large", but you can change the recording destination in the app settings.
- Voice command to end memo recording (default: "finish") can be changed in app settings

</details>

#### Recording from Apple Watch

Speak to your Apple Watch, and the text is added to the memo you chose in "Voice Widget Memo" on your iPhone. You can take notes without taking out your iPhone (watchOS 11 or later).

##### Preparation for use

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Setup steps</summary>
<br>

1. **Install the app on your Apple Watch**: On your iPhone, open the Watch app → "Voice Widget Memo" → tap "Install" (if automatic app install is on, it is already installed)
2. **Choose the memo to add to**: In "Voice Widget Memo" Settings → "Memo for Apple Watch", choose "Home Screen: Large", "Home Screen: Medium", "Home Screen: Small", "Lock Screen: Square", or "Lock Screen: 1 Line" (the default is "Home Screen: Large")
3. **Add it to your watch face (recommended)**: Touch and hold the watch face → "Edit" → select a complication slot and choose "Voice Widget Memo"

</details>

##### How to Use

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Usage Flow</summary>
<br>

1. Tap "Voice Widget Memo" on your watch face. Dictation starts right away
2. When you finish speaking, tap "Done". You can also double tap with your thumb and index finger (Apple Watch Series 9 / Ultra 2 or later)
3. When "Added to ..." appears, you're done. To keep speaking, tap "Speak" or double tap

To return to the watch face, press the Digital Crown or lower your wrist.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Good to know</summary>

- **The iPhone widget may not update right away**: The text has been added to the memo. iOS limits how often widgets are redrawn, so the widget may update later. Open "Voice Widget Memo" to update it immediately
- **When your iPhone is recording**: "Your iPhone is recording. The text will be added when recording stops." appears. The text is added after recording on your iPhone ends
- **When your iPhone is not nearby**: "The text will be added when it reaches your iPhone." appears. The text is added in the order you spoke when your iPhone connects
- **An "Apple Watch Keyboard" notification appears on your iPhone**: iOS shows this notification when you start entering text on your Apple Watch. "Voice Widget Memo" cannot turn it off
- **Siri on Apple Watch is not supported**: If you ask Siri on your Apple Watch, Apple's Notes app opens instead. On Apple Watch, open "Voice Widget Memo" from its icon or complication
- **To return to the watch face as soon as you lower your wrist**: On your Apple Watch, go to Settings → General → Return to Clock, and change the time for "Voice Widget Memo"

</details>

#### FAQ

<details markdown="1">
<summary>About backups</summary>
<br>

A backup of a memo is created at the following times (only when the memo has changed):

- When you tap the save button
- When you stop recording
- When you close the app (go back to the home screen)
- When text is added from Apple Watch

![backup_save_button](images/us/backup_save_button.png)

##### Where backups are saved

Backups are saved in two places.

| Location | When the app is deleted |
|---|---|
| Local | Backup files are also deleted |
| iCloud | Backup files are not deleted |

##### About backup data

For both local and iCloud, the latest 20 backups are kept for each type of memo (when there are more than 20, the oldest ones are deleted). The file name ends with the date and time.

| Type of memo | Backup file name (example) |
|---|---|
| Home Screen: Large | backup_large_20241220112233.txt |
| Home Screen: Medium | backup_medium_20241220112233.txt |
| Home Screen: Small | backup_small_20241220112233.txt |
| Lock Screen: Square | backup_lockLarge_20241220112233.txt |
| Lock Screen: 1 Line | backup_lockSmall_20241220112233.txt |

##### How to check backups (local)

Backups are saved inside the "Voice Widget Memo" app. If you delete the app, the backup files are also deleted. Open the "Files" app → "On My iPhone" → "KoeMemo" to see them.

##### How to check backups (iCloud)

Backups are saved in iCloud and remain even if you delete the app. Open the "Files" app → "iCloud Drive" → "KoeMemo" to see them.

If backups are not saved to iCloud, check that iCloud is turned on for "Voice Widget Memo" in the Settings app.

</details>
